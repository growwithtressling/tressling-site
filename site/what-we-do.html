What We Do

The work behind your next stage of growth

Tressling works with owners and management teams to turn business decisions into practical changes. An engagement may focus on a specific opportunity or extend from the initial strategy through implementation and expansion.

We agree on the work, responsibilities, and intended results together. Our partners combine business, capital, technology, and commercial expertise around the priorities of your business.

Area of work · 01

Business strategy and transformation

We examine how AI is changing customer needs, competitive alternatives, and the economics of your business. Together, we identify where your capabilities can create value and decide which opportunities warrant investment.

The work can include customer and market assessment, evaluation of new offers, an operating plan, and a sequence of initiatives with clear ownership. The strategy connects the business you operate today with the one you intend to build.

Area of work · 02

Capital planning and financing

We develop financial models that connect commercial assumptions with delivery capacity, cash flow, and investment requirements. We assess what the existing business can contribute and the additional funding required at each stage.

Our work includes evaluating funding options, preparing the business case, structuring debt and equity financing, and working with ownership through discussions with potential capital providers. Funding requirements are reviewed as operating results develop.

Area of work · 03

People and operating performance

We work with leadership and the people closest to the work to understand how the business actually operates. Together, we clarify roles, examine handoffs and decisions, and redesign the processes that matter to delivery and performance.

Implementation combines manageable changes with clear communication, practical training, and regular feedback. The aim is to build capability and make the new way of working part of everyday operations.

Area of work · 04

Technology strategy and implementation

We connect business requirements with the technology needed to deliver them. The work begins with existing systems, available data, integration requirements, and the capabilities your team will need to operate the solution.

Joel Massicotte, a founder and partner of Tressling.ai, brings engineering and technology leadership to this work. His expertise covers architecture, platform and vendor selection, systems integration, AI product development, and modernization of existing technology. Testing, security, user adoption, and ongoing ownership are considered as part of delivery.

Area of work · 05

Positioning and commercial execution

A new or evolving offer needs a clear place in the market. We work on identifying the customers who will value it, explaining why it matters, and connecting that positioning to commercial activity.

Ivana Johnston, a founder and partner of Tressling.ai, brings market positioning and commercial expertise to this work. Her capabilities include go-to-market strategy, branding and messaging, demand generation, thought leadership, and public relations. The work is shaped around the audience, offer, and commercial objectives of the engagement.

Area of work · 06

Performance review and scaling

We establish measures and review results with management as the work progresses. Customer response, delivery capacity, earnings, and cash flow provide the evidence for decisions about what to refine and where to invest next.

As demand grows, we assess the people, processes, technology, and capital required to serve it. Scaling includes strengthening customer acquisition, onboarding, and consistent delivery so that new demand develops into lasting revenue.

How we choose

How we choose the companies we work with

A business worth building on. Customers who value what it does, and a team with capabilities worth keeping.

A clear need to transform. AI is changing the market, and the owners or board see it.

A place for our expertise. The owners or board want to add strategy, operations, technology or capital experience alongside the management team.

Results we agree on. Earnings, cash flow, new revenue, and how we will measure them.
