FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers to the questions owners and boards ask us most.

Who does the work?

Dan McLaughlin leads each engagement. Joel Massicotte and Ivana Johnston join when their skills and experience are needed.

What roles do you take?

We work alongside the owner and the board, in a fractional executive role or as members of an advisory board, working to enhance the capabilities of your team.

How are you paid?

We start with a free discovery call to see if we can add value. From there, we tailor our engagement to achieve your goals. Each stage has a defined scope, timeline and deliverables, with a fee to match. Our success fees are tied to the value we help create.

Do you invest in the companies you work with?

No. We advise on capital needs and financing. We do not invest.

Are you independent of vendors?

Yes. We take no fees or commissions from vendors.

How many companies do you work with?

No more than four at a time, so each one gets our full attention.

What industries do you work in?

Our experience covers the construction industry value chain, real estate, manufacturing, technology, and hospitality and travel.

What is the first step?

A discovery call. It’s free, and it tells us both whether we can add value.
